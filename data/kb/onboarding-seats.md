# Seats, Roles and Onboarding

Roles are Owner, Admin, Member and Viewer. Only Owners can change billing or
delete the workspace. Admins manage users and security settings.

Seats are billed per active user per month. Adding a seat mid-cycle is prorated.
Removing a seat credits the remainder of the cycle against the next invoice.

Invitations expire after 14 days. An expired invitation must be resent; the link
cannot be extended.

Guest users on Business and Enterprise plans do not consume a seat but are
limited to projects they are explicitly added to.
