# Invoices, Receipts and Tax

Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month and emailed to the billing
contact. You can download any past invoice from Settings > Billing > History.

To add a VAT or GST number, go to Settings > Billing > Tax Details. Invoices
issued before the tax ID was added cannot be reissued with it retroactively.

Purchase orders are supported on Business and Enterprise plans only. Send the
PO to ap@example.com and reference the workspace ID.

Failed payments are retried on day 1, day 3 and day 7. After the third failure
the workspace moves to read-only until payment succeeds.
