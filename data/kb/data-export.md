# Data Export and Deletion

Workspace owners can export all workspace data from Settings > Data > Export.
Large workspaces are processed asynchronously and emailed as a download link
valid for 72 hours.

Deleting a workspace starts a 30-day soft-delete window during which an owner
can restore it. After 30 days the data is purged from primary storage and from
backups within a further 35 days.

Individual record deletion is immediate in the application and propagates to
analytics within 24 hours.

We do not sell customer data and do not train models on workspace content.
