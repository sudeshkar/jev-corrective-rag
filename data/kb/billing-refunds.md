# Refunds and Billing Adjustments

We issue a full refund for any subscription cancelled within 14 days of the
initial charge. After 14 days, refunds are prorated to the remaining days in
the billing period.

Annual plans are refundable within 30 days. After 30 days we issue account
credit rather than a card refund.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method and take 5-10 business
days to appear. We cannot refund to a different card than the one charged.

Enterprise contracts are governed by the signed order form, not this policy.
Route Enterprise refund requests to the account manager.
